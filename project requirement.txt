School management system 
Customer-facing features
User account & profile
•	Register/login (via email, otp, google/facebook)
•	Manage student/parent profile (personal details, contact info, student’s academic data)
•	View academic history (grades, attendance, timetable)
•	Add or manage multiple students (for parents with more than one child)
•	Manage communication preferences (receive notifications about assignments, grades, events)
Course & subject browsing
•	Browse by categories: subjects, grade levels, extracurricular activities
•	Filter by:
o	Grade level (e.g., primary, secondary, higher secondary)
o	Subject (e.g., mathematics, science, history, languages)
o	Teacher (e.g., search by subject teacher for private sessions)
o	Time slot availability (e.g., morning or afternoon sessions)
•	Smart search with auto-suggestions (search by subject, teacher, or grade)
Timetable & schedule
•	View class schedules (students can view their daily or weekly class schedules)
•	View teacher availability (parents/students can view available times for teacher meetings)
•	Track extracurricular activities (sports, music, clubs, etc.)
•	Set reminders for classes/exams/assignments (push notifications or email alerts)
Student details page
•	High-res images (student photo)
•	Academic details (grades, assignments, progress reports, subject-wise performance)
•	Attendance record (daily attendance, leave requests, sick leave)
•	Behavior & conduct reports (teacher evaluations, discipline records)
•	Notifications & messages from teachers (remarks, updates, and alerts)
•	View results and exams (marks, grade point average, and rankings)
Fees & payment
•	View fee structure (tuition fees, extracurricular charges, exam fees, etc.)
•	Pay fees online (via payment gateways like stripe, razorpay, upi, credit/debit cards)
•	Track payment history (previous fees, upcoming dues, invoices)
•	Apply for financial aid (for students with scholarships or fee waivers)
•	Set up recurring payments (automatic payments for monthly fees)
•	Generate payment receipts (print or email receipts after payments)
Notifications & alerts
•	Receive notifications (for assignments, upcoming exams, grade updates)
•	Sms/email alerts (for emergency school closures, events, parent-teacher meetings)
•	Push notifications (for school events, activities, class changes)
Student attendance
•	Mark attendance (students mark attendance or can view attendance for the day)
•	View attendance history (students and parents can track attendance rates and absences)
•	Request leave (students/parents can submit leave requests for specific dates)
•	Notification of absences (automatic notifications if a student is marked absent)

Admin panel features
Student & enrollment management
•	Add/edit/delete student profiles (basic details, contact info, academic history)
•	Manage student admissions (register new students, transfer students)
•	Track student progress (grades, attendance, report cards)
•	Assign subjects & classes (link students to specific subjects, teachers, and grades)
•	Assign extracurricular activities (manage club memberships, sports teams, etc.)

Teacher & staff management
•	Add/edit/delete teacher profiles (teacher details, subjects taught, schedule)
•	Assign teaching subjects (link teachers to relevant subjects and classes)
•	Manage staff schedules (view and update staff working hours, class schedules)
•	Track staff performance (e.g., teacher evaluations, training records)
Class & subject management
•	Create/manage courses and subjects (assign subjects to specific grade levels)
•	Create/assign class schedules (assign teachers to time slots and classrooms)
•	Manage class divisions (e.g., a, b, c for a specific grade)
Attendance & leave management
•	Mark student attendance (for each class or day)
•	Track teacher attendance (manage leave requests, sick days, and absences)
•	Manage leave approvals (approve/deny leave requests for students and teachers)
•	Generate attendance reports (for students, classes, and teachers)
Exams & grading
•	Create/manage exams (assignments, quizzes, midterms, final exams)
•	Assign grading criteria (e.g., letter grades, marks out of 100)
•	Grade submission (teachers can input grades for exams and assignments)
•	Generate report cards (automatically generate grade sheets for students)
•	Track student performance (analyze results, identify students in need of support)
•	Publish results (publish grades for exams, assignments, or term results)
Fee management
•	Set fee structure (tuition fees, activity charges, exam fees, etc.)
•	Track student fee payments (view payment status, generate invoices, mark payments as received)
•	Create and manage payment schedules (due dates for term fees, monthly installments)
•	Generate fee receipts (for each payment made by parents)
•	Send payment reminders (email/sms notifications for upcoming payments)
•	Generate financial reports (track income, outstanding fees, financial aid, scholarships)
Parent-teacher communication
•	Manage parent-teacher meetings (schedule and track appointments)
•	Send notifications (upcoming meetings, academic progress reports, behavior feedback)
•	Enable messaging (secure messaging between parents and teachers)
•	View meeting histories (track past meetings, notes, follow-up actions)
Events & calendar management
•	Manage school events (sports day, field trips, cultural events)
•	Create event schedules (time, date, location, participants)
•	Track event attendance (students, teachers, and parents can rsvp)
•	Publish events calendar (for parents, students, and staff to view school events)
Analytics & reporting
•	Generate student performance reports (grades, attendance, behavior)
•	Monitor school-wide performance (exam results, attendance rates)
•	Track teacher performance (grades, feedback from students, and evaluations)
•	Financial reports (school income, expenses, fee collections)
